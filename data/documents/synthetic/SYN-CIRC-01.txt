Circular No. ACAD/SYN/2026-27/01
Office of the Dean (Academics)
Subject: Revised minimum attendance requirement for B.Tech programmes
Effective 1 January 2027. This circular supersedes clause 11.2 of the Academic Rules and Regulations of NSUT.

1. Revised attendance requirement
With effect from 1 January 2027, a B.Tech student must have a minimum of 80% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear in the Mid Semester and End Semester Examinations of that subject.

2. Relaxation in attendance under clauses 11.3 to 11.6 of the Academic Rules and Regulations remains unchanged.
